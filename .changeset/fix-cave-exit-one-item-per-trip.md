"haulers-dream": patch
---

Fix colonists ordered out of a cave with the loot carrying one insect jelly at a time (#243). Telling pawns to leave through a cave exit and take items with them had each of them pick a single item off a stack, walk it to the exit, come back, and do it again, for an enormous amount of walking and almost no hauling. Turning Hauler's Dream off stopped it and turning it back on brought it straight back, which is exactly right. This one was ours.

It is the same bug as the "one hauler making ten trips where two would have done" fix from the last release, in a case that fix did not reach. When several pawns are all told to load the same thing, the mod shares the pile out between them, and it works that share out afresh at the start of every trip — so when the sharing is wrong, every trip is smaller than the one before it. The earlier fix added the rule that stops that: if everything still waiting already fits in one trip, do not divide it at all, just carry it. But that rule has to know how much a pawn can carry in one trip, and it quietly did nothing at all for a pawn that has no weight limit to speak of. If you have Smart overload at its far-left stop, "No slowdown — carry freely", and no "Max carry weight" cap set, that is precisely your colonists: no ceiling of any kind. So the pile was divided again on every trip, each trip carrying three-quarters of what the last one carried, until it reached the floor the mod keeps so that a pawn can always claim at least one of the heaviest thing waiting — and that floor is one item. Two hundred insect jelly and four colonists came out as fifty, then thirty-seven, twenty-eight, twenty-one, and on down to seven trips carrying a single jelly each: nineteen trips in all, where RimWorld on its own would have hand-carried a full stack in one.

Two things changed. A pawn with no weight ceiling still makes trips, so when the mod asks whether a pile is big enough to be worth sharing out, it now measures a trip as one ordinary packful instead of treating it as bottomless. And the sharing itself now refuses to divide anything at all when it is told the pawn has no limit, as a backstop for the same mistake being made anywhere else: sharing a load out can only ever make a trip smaller, never bigger, so declining to share is the only safe way for that decision to be wrong. One pawn clearing the whole order in a single trip is a better outcome than four pawns making nineteen shrinking ones, every time.

Nothing changes for a pawn that has a real weight limit — which is every other position of the Smart overload slider, including the default, along with "Max carry weight" and strict carry weight — nor for pods, shuttles and vehicles, which carry a mass capacity of their own that always gave the rule a number to work with. A crew loading a large shuttle together still divides a big order between themselves exactly as it did before.
