feat: bulk-unload transporters. Transport pods, shuttles and modded transporters with cargo gain a "Bulk unload all" toggle that keeps hauling colonists emptying the hold, one backpack-filling trip at a time, several pawns in parallel, until nothing is left, instead of vanilla's dump-everything-on-the-floor unload. While it is on, a right-click "Prioritize bulk unloading" order sends one hauler that keeps returning until the hold is done, prioritizing the shuttle over other work. Loading and unloading exclude each other: HD never touches a hold while cargo is still being gathered into it, and a flagged hold refuses new loads. Passengers are never pulled out.
