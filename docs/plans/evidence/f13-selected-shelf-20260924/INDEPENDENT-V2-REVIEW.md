## Finding: an incomplete pooled scan permanently refuses a free selected shelf

Priority P2. `v2/source/Source/HaulersDream/StorageCommitments.ExplicitShelf.cs`, lines103–104 and130–132, discards the `FreeUnitsFor` truncation output. Observe refuses zero before it even checks the selected shelf. Acquire also derives zero units from that incomplete value.

`StorageCommitments.MeasureGroupUncached`, lines843–905, inspects at most200 cells. The actual native `StorageGroup.CellsList` concatenates member slot cells in member order (`native-source/StorageGroup.cs.txt`, property CellsList). A linked native group can therefore have its first200 cells full, forbidden or otherwise unavailable, followed by the selected shelf with real free room. The scan returns zero with Truncated=true; direct selected-cell observation would succeed, but both new admission paths permanently refuse. No advancing tick changes the deterministic scan order. This is the exact case warned about by FreeUnitsFor's contract: a caller must not refuse on a truncated underestimate.

Recommended correction: include the selected shelf's actual cells in a bounded measurement, or establish a conservative selected-shelf physical lower bound, while retaining ordinary same-def and cross-def shared claim charges and actual native/incoming exclusions. Do not bypass the shared ledger by replacing zero with physical space, and do not turn this into an unbounded whole-map scan. A focused native regression should place200 blocked pooled cells before the free selected shelf, then preserve a genuine ordinary competing claim in that group to prove the correction still subtracts committed cargo. Root assigned the narrow correction and regression to the author. Original v2 remains frozen.
