Compare the same record in LoadedGame before any native tick. For this first chain, remainder lists should be genuinely empty; preserve that expectation rather than fabricating recovery pieces. Keep the numeric source history and actual source reference distinct.

2. **Make each transfer receipt a physical before/after observation.** `Picked` logs the product helper's `piece` and requested count; `PlaceAfter` logs the product's delivered counter. `placements.Count >= 2` consequently counts helper calls, including zero-progress attempts, rather than proving two productive placements. Capture immutable exact source/hands/private-owner/destination identities and counts before the helper, then read actual native ownership/counts afterward. Require source reduction to equal the exact new held piece, and destination growth to equal the physical parcel reduction and credited increment. Retain actual exception values. In the continuation, count only positive physical placements toward the multi-trip requirement. The producer's first pickup is already part of the original checkpoint and must remain bound to that checkpoint's receipt.

3. **Close the physical conservation boundary at completion and stability.** Source34 + destination116 and personal9 do not alone exclude an extra piece left in hands/private recovery or at another map cell. Retain the original personal stack identity as well as its quantity. Add empty hands and empty private-recovery assertions at terminal completion, and a selected-def census that explicitly accounts for pre-existing foreign stock. Preserve the original source identities and exact destination; any newly created selected-def stock outside those locations must fail. Recheck during the existing300-tick stability window and the final pre-tick reload. Do not delete ordinary Quicktest Steel or silently exclude it: record/verify its starting identity/count, or use an explicitly owned parked baseline.

The source is never replaced by a nearby same-def stack. Original forbidden authority is explicit, and a newly forbidden previously allowed source blocks before pickup. Hands capacity comes from native `Pawn_CarryTracker.MaxStackSpaceEver`; personal inventory and Keep are not intake authority.

The finalization postfix therefore reconciles actual resolved jobs before the fixture's pre-tick snapshot; the later native PauseOnLoad single tick remains separate. Existing nearby/transporter/F38 paths are retained. Bare-cell admission excludes storage/provider cells and uses the native reservation domain; it does not create an explicit-only capacity ledger.
