# F02 — crafting with the pawn's own inventory

Resolved on 24 September 2026 in commit `096edbc`, as a verified support answer plus a demonstrated ordinary-bill permission fix. The published mod already consumes eligible HD-tracked carried ingredients. The reporter's exact historical save and cause were not reproduced; a recurring general inability to craft from inventory is not established.

C424 subject S05 asks whether crafting from inventory is supported, because the shared-inventory description implied it but the reporter's pawns did not use their own stock. Ordinary bills use HD-tracked hauling stock when the crafting-sharing setting is enabled. Untracked personal items are outside that injection path. The revised README, compatibility guide and single tooltip key in all 16 languages now explain that boundary, Keep as an unloading quantity, and Common Sense gathering ownership. Construction retains its separate broader policy; explicit batch crafting has its own driver and permission setting.

Verification exposed a separate concrete defect: ordinary bill candidate injection admitted forbidden carried stock, even though the native floor search rejects it. With forbidden tracked Milk40 and permitted Rice20/Potatoes20 available, the old candidate repeatedly selected the milk and reached RimWorld's repeated-job error. `InventoryShare.AddCarrierStacks` now checks the exact requesting worker's native `IsForbidden` permission before accepting a carried ingredient. Both self and eligible other carriers use that gate. Native exceptions, recipe filters, reservations, reachability and other eligibility rules remain unchanged.
