## Verified mechanism and proposal assessment

1. `Verse.UnfinishedThing.cs.txt` reads `boundBillInt.DeletedOrDereferenced` both in BoundBill's getter and during Saving. `RimWorld.Bill.cs.txt:111` dereferences `billStack.billGiver` when the bill is not marked deleted. A live bill with null stack therefore throws. `Verse.AI.HaulAIUtility.cs.txt:94` actually consults BoundBill in automatic hauling; this is not merely a UI-label failure.
2. Bill's stack is unsaved. Native BillStack assigns it in AddBill and in **ResolvingCrossRefs**, before the proposed PostLoadInit guard. Thus normal native on-stack bills already have their stack when the guard runs. A separately deep-saved bill outside a stack can retain its unfinished-item reference while its unsaved stack remains null. The precise duplicate-load-ID behavior described by the contributor is not required to establish this null-stack crash and should not be claimed reproduced by a simpler fixture.
3. The proposal's `TryUnbind` reads the raw UFT bill field, avoids the unsafe getter, clears that reference and clears the reciprocal BoundUft only when it points to this item. Native BoundUft and ClearBoundUft are plain field access. Recipe, creator, stuff, workLeft and ingredient list are untouched. Healthy non-null-stack bindings and unrelated reciprocal bindings are left alone.
4. Native WorkGiver_DoBill can resume a loose UFT, but the condition is narrower than “any matching bill”: the original creator must be the pawn, the recipe must match, ingredients must satisfy the current bill filter, and normal reachability/reservation/skill/work gates apply. Toils_Recipe initializes driver work from the existing UFT.workLeft and updates it during work. The repair must preserve those native eligibility rules rather than reassigning the creator or inventing a new bill.
5. PostLoadInit handles normally scribed UFTs, including held items; Saving protects the actual dangerous vanilla pre-save read. A once-per-load haulable-map sweep covers spawned subclasses that skip base ExposeData. It does not prove coverage for every unscribed/custom-contained third-party subclass. Keep this as a bounded backstop, not an all-mod compatibility claim. Preserve field-binding failure isolation and visible diagnostics; do not wrap the actual repair in silent exception suppression.
