## Important historical regression to retest

Eversset’s unlocked topic describes multiple haulers each responding to a tiny remaining storage capacity, then returning surplus after the first pawn fills it. It explicitly reports persistence after **v1.22.0**, but was opened **August 7**, before the August 9 v1.24.0 fix. The two August 17 replies do not renew that reproduction: one reports the BWM-related exception and one is hidden. [T01-OP](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3742459652/582804662258990241/) [T01-R01](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3742459652/582804662258990241/#c418424310691114229) [T01-R02](https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3742459652/582804662258990241/#c418424310691115322)

Keep concurrent destination-capacity accounting on the regression-test list because the report is detailed and an earlier fix was insufficient. Do not label it confirmed broken on v1.24.0 from the topic’s last-activity date alone. The separate July transport-loading request makes the analogous case for incoming carried cargo and asks to preserve opportunistic loading while preventing duplicate diversions. The developer offered v1.18.0 for retesting; no reporter confirmation follows in that topic.
